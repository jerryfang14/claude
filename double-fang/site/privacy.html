Privacy policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

The short version

  • We only collect what you send us: your name, your email and your message.
  • We use it to reply to you. That is all.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, no mailing list. We never sell your details.
  • You can ask us to show, fix or delete your details at any time. Write to jerrydfang@gmail.com.

1. Who we are

Double Fang is a small studio based in Ontario, Canada. We build websites, automations and AI tools for small businesses. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Double Fang.

One person at Double Fang is responsible for how we handle personal information. You can reach them at jerrydfang@gmail.com.

2. What we collect and why

When you use the contact form

The form asks for your name, your email address, a topic and an optional message. If you pressed a button that carries your estimate or your picked tasks into the form, those lines are added to the message, where you can read and edit them first.

The form does not send anything to our servers. It opens your own email app with the note filled in, and nothing reaches us until you press send there. We then receive it as a normal email.

We use these details to reply to you and, if you ask for one, to prepare a quote.

When you email us directly

We receive your email address and whatever you write.

When you become a client

We collect what we need to do the work and to invoice you, such as your business name and billing details. We agree on this with you directly.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for your phone number or home address on this site. We do not take card payments on this site. We do not track your location or build an advertising profile.

3. Cookies and browser storage

This site sets no cookies. It saves two small items in your browser's local storage: your theme (light or dark) and your privacy choice. They stay on your device and are never sent to us. The cookie policy lists them.

There are no analytics, no advertising trackers and no social media pixels on this site. Fonts and icons are served from our own site, so your browser does not contact a font service or any other outside company when you visit.

4. Hosting logs

Like every website, ours is delivered by a hosting company. It keeps standard technical logs, such as IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time, to keep the site running and secure. We do not use these logs to identify you.

5. Who we share it with

  • Our email provider. Our inbox is run by Google (Gmail). Emails you send us are stored there.
  • Our hosting provider. It delivers this site and keeps the logs described above.
  • Tools for your project. If we work together, we may use other services to build what you asked for. We tell you which ones first.

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We only share it beyond this list if the law requires us to.

Some of these providers store information outside Canada, including in the United States. While it is there, it is subject to the laws of that country.

6. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not turn into a project are deleted within 12 months of our last message.
  • Client records are kept while we work together. Invoices and tax records are kept for at least six years, as Canadian tax law requires.

You can ask us to delete your details sooner. The next section explains how.

7. Your choices and rights

Under Canada's privacy law (PIPEDA), you can:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you and get a copy,
  • ask us to correct it,
  • withdraw your consent and ask us to delete it,
  • ask how we have used it and who we have shared it with.

There is no fee. We answer within 30 days. We may ask a question first, to make sure the request really comes from you.

If you are not happy with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Delete my data

  1. Email jerrydfang@gmail.com from the address you used with us, or use the contact form and pick "Delete my data".
  2. We check that the request is yours, then delete your details from our inbox and our files.
  3. We write back within 30 days to say it is done. If the law requires us to keep something, such as an invoice, we tell you what and why.

8. If you live outside Canada

We are a Canadian studio, but anyone can visit this site.

  • European Union and United Kingdom. You also have the right to object to how we use your information or ask us to restrict it, to receive it in a portable format, and to complain to your local data protection authority. We use your information because you asked us to get in touch, which is a step before a possible contract, and because we have a legitimate interest in answering enquiries.
  • California. We do not sell or share personal information, as those words are defined in California law. You can ask what we hold, ask us to correct it and ask us to delete it. We will not treat you differently for asking.

9. Children

This site is for business owners. It is not meant for anyone under 18, and the contact form asks you to confirm your age. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, tell us and we will delete it.

10. Marketing email

We do not run a mailing list. We only email you to answer you or to do work you asked for. If we ever start a newsletter, you will have to opt in first. Every email will say who it is from and carry an unsubscribe link that works, as Canada's anti-spam law (CASL) requires.

11. Keeping it safe

Only the people at Double Fang who need your details can see them, and we keep them with established providers. No system is perfectly safe. If a breach ever creates a real risk of significant harm to you, we will tell you and the Privacy Commissioner, as Canadian law requires.

12. Changes to this policy

When something changes, we update this page and the date at the top. If the change is a big one, we also say so on the home page.
