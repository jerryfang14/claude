Refund policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

The short version

  • Changed your mind before we start? Full refund.
  • After we start, you pay only for finished stages.
  • Monthly plans cancel anytime, with no fee.
  • No hidden fees. Every cost is on your quote before you say yes.

1. Before work starts

If you change your mind before work starts, we refund everything you have paid. You do not need to give a reason. Work starts on the start date written in your quote.

2. After work starts

Every project is split into stages, and each stage has its own price on your quote. You can stop at any time.

  • You pay for the stages we have finished and delivered.
  • You do not pay for a stage that is not finished.
  • If you have paid more than the finished stages add up to, we refund the difference.
  • Finished stages that you have paid for stay yours.

3. Monthly plans

Some clients keep us on a monthly plan to look after what we built. Plans run month to month.

  • You can cancel at any time. One line by email is enough.
  • There is no cancellation fee and no notice period.
  • The plan ends at the end of the month you have already paid for. We do not bill you again.
  • We tell you before any price change, and you can cancel instead.

4. Costs paid to other companies

Some builds need things bought from other companies, such as a domain name, hosting or a software subscription. These are listed on your quote before we buy anything. Once bought, they are that company's to refund, not ours. We will help you ask them.

5. How to cancel or ask for a refund

  1. Email jerrydfang@gmail.com, or use the contact form and pick "Cancel a plan or ask for a refund".
  2. Tell us which project or plan it is about.
  3. We reply within one business day to confirm.
  4. Refunds go back the same way you paid, within 10 business days of our confirmation.
