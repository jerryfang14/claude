The short version

  • This site sets no cookies.
  • It saves two small items in your browser, so it can remember your theme and your privacy choice.
  • No tracking, no ads, no analytics.
  • You can change your choice at any time.

1. What is saved in your browser

Instead of cookies, this site uses your browser's local storage. These items stay on your device and are never sent to us.

NameWhat it doesHow long it stays
df-themeRemembers whether you picked the light or dark theme. It is only saved if you change the theme.Until you clear your browser data.
df-consentRemembers your privacy choice and the date you made it, so we do not ask on every visit.12 months. After that, we ask again.

Both exist only to remember settings you chose. Neither is used to track you.

2. Analytics and tracking

We do not run analytics, advertising trackers or social media pixels on this site today.

If we ever add analytics, they will stay switched off unless you pick "Allow analytics". If you pick "Essential only", nothing extra loads.

3. Other companies

No other company sets cookies through this site. Fonts and icons are served from our own site.

4. Change your choice

You can reopen the choice at any time with the button below, or with "Privacy choices" at the bottom of every page. You can also clear both items in your browser's settings, under site data.
