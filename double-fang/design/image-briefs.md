# Image briefs (for later, when an image tool is connected)

The site ships with no image files. Every visual is the logo or real interface drawn in code. That is on purpose: nothing to license, nothing that could pass for a fake photo, and the pages stay fast. These are the upgrades worth making first if you want pictures later.

All of them share one world: a bright open sky in sky blue (#0099FF), soft cloud-white (#FDFDFE) light, clean matte objects, and a single amber red (#FA2406) detail per picture. Calm and minimal. No text, no logos, no real brand marks, no people's faces unless listed.

1. **Example build covers** (Things we can build, 6 images, 5:4, 1200x960). One quiet object per row, floating in front of the sky with a soft shadow below it: a paper appointment book with one red ribbon (Booking agent), a matte white compass (Lead engine), a tiny shopfront awning (Store in a week), a letter tray with one sheet lifted (Inbox triage), a folded weekly report (Monday report), a film reel on its side (Content engine). Object centered low, top 40% empty sky. All six get the same treatment. These would replace the drawn mock-ups that follow the cursor, so keep the drawn ones unless the pictures are clearly better.
2. **Founder portrait** (Final call, 4:5, 1200x1500). Only if the founder wants a face on the site, and only a real photo of the real person. Three-quarter view, plain light top, daylight from a window, sky blue backdrop out of focus.
3. **Social share image** (og:image, 1200x630). The current one is drawn by `design/tools/icons.mjs`: the mark and the headline on a white card in front of the sky. Replace it only if a photo version reads better at thumbnail size.

Rules for anything added later:
- Write real alt text for every picture that carries meaning. Leave it empty for decoration.
- Keep a note of where each picture came from and its license on `site/licenses.html`.
- Never present a generated picture as a photo of real work, a real client or a real result.
