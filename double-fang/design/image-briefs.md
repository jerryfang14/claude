# Image briefs (for when an image platform is connected)

The current page ships with no photography: every visual is the mark or real UI. These are the upgrades worth generating first. All share one world: off-black #0B0C0E studio, soft warm key light from upper left, a single ember (#FF5B37) rim or reflection, fine film grain, calm and minimal. No text, no logos, no real brand marks.

1. **Example build covers** (Things we can build, 6 images, 5:4, 1200x960). One quiet object per card on the dark studio set: a paper appointment book with a single ember ribbon (Booking agent); a brass-free matte black compass on slate (Lead engine); a small shopfront awning in miniature (Store in a week); a stacked letter tray with one sheet glowing (Inbox triage); a folded weekly report on a desk, soft shadow (Monday report); a film reel on its side (Content engine). Object centered low, top 40% empty. All six get equal treatment.
2. **Founder portrait** (Final call, 4:5, 1200x1500), only if the founder wants a face on the site: seated, three-quarter view, dark knit, same key light, ember rim on the shoulder, background out of focus.
3. **Social share image** (og:image, 1200x630): the Double Fang mark in ember and bone on the dark studio surface, small, centered, with generous empty space.
